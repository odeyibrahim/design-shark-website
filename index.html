Where your design gets made.

Our own production floor, in Lagos. The team that cuts and checks your sample is the team that makes your batch, so there is no chain of agents between you and the people sewing your pieces. A 500-machine smart factory is in development, built to take this same standard to scale.

Where we’re going.

Design Shark International is a garment house at its core — and a fashion technology company by design. The vision: to be Africa’s pioneer fashion technology company, built on five businesses that share one standard of quality.

01

DSI Technology

Mezer, our AI body-measurement app, is the MVP of a full 3D Fashion Design Software Suite — designs move from sketch to 3D, so size and fit are settled before a piece is cut. Every scan also feeds a standardized African size chart.

02

DSI Manufacturing

A 500-machine smart factory for Lagos, in development with our partners Wazir Advisors — world-class capacity, powered by AI, and a faster, cheaper alternative to sending production abroad.

03

DSI Fashion Designer Search

The talent search that drew 500+ applications in its first month returns as a television series, travelling all six geopolitical zones to find and scale marketable designers.

04

DSI Retail

A data-driven e-commerce platform for standardized, locally made fashion — with AI and AR virtual try-on and size prediction. In development.

05

DSI Faculty of Fashion

An online platform teaching the fashion workforce digitalized production methods, built on partnerships with fashion schools and technical academies.
